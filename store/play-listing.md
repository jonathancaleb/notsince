## Full description  (limit 4000)

```
notsince counts the days since you stopped. That is the whole idea.

Pick something you are stepping away from — drinking, smoking, vaping, a habit
you would rather not have — and notsince keeps the tally from the moment you
began. No sign-up, no account, no setup beyond naming the thing.

A COUNT, NOT A SCORE

Most trackers turn quitting into a game you can lose. notsince does not. There
are no points, no leaderboards and no streak to break. If you slip, the count
simply starts again: the run you had moves into your history where you can
still see it, and a new one begins. Nothing turns red and nothing tells you off.

WATCH IT GROW

Every habit has a plant. It starts as a seed on day one and gains a stage each
time you pass a milestone — a sprout at 24 hours, leaves through the first
weeks and months, a flower at a year. Milestones are yours to set: keep the
defaults or choose your own.

A REAL ALARM

notsince includes a proper alarm clock, not a reminder. It rings through silent
mode, shows a full-screen wake-up over the lock screen, and survives a restart.
Set it once or repeat it on the days you choose, pick from four built-in
sounds, and snooze without fumbling.

NOTES AND TASKS

Somewhere to put the things that go with it. Write a note to keep, or a task to
finish and tick off. Give each one a colour, and set a time on a task if you
want a nudge when it passes.

QUIET NOTIFICATIONS

A short line when a habit reaches a milestone, and a nudge if a task goes past
its time. Both can be turned off, separately, and neither is loud — the alarm
is the loud thing, and it has its own switch.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR PHONE

There is no account, no cloud, no sync and no analytics. notsince makes no
network requests at all: everything you write is stored on your device and
nowhere else. That is a property of how the app is built, not a promise about
how we behave. Settings has a single button that erases everything.

ALSO IN THE BOX

• Track more than one habit and swipe between them
• Backdate a start time if you began before you installed this
• A history of every past run and how long each one lasted
• Light and dark themes, or follow your phone
• Free, with no ads and nothing to buy

notsince is a self-tracking tool, not medical advice or treatment. If you are
managing a dependency, please talk to a qualified professional.

Questions or bugs: sabilacalebj@gmail.com
```

## Notes on wording

Play rejects listings for a few things this copy deliberately avoids:

- **No superlatives or rankings** — nothing claims "best", "#1" or "top".
- **No medical or treatment claims.** The app touches dependency, which is a
  sensitive area; the closing line states plainly that it is not medical advice
  and points elsewhere. Removing that line raises the risk of a rejection under
  the health-claims policy.
- **No testimonials, no fake urgency, no keyword stuffing.**
- **The section headings are capitalised, not shouting a whole sentence** —
  Play's metadata policy flags full-sentence ALL CAPS.

The privacy paragraph is worded to match what the Data safety form must say. If
one changes, change the other, or the listing contradicts the declaration.
