Privacy policy

Last updated 4 September 2026 · Package app.notsince

notsince collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing outside your device. There is no account to create and no server to talk to.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — the habits you track, their start times, milestones and history, your alarms, and your notes and tasks — is written to a database file inside the app's own private storage on your phone. Android prevents other apps from reading it.

What the app sends

Nothing. notsince makes no network requests. It contains no advertising, no crash reporting, no usage analytics and no third-party tracking libraries. Fonts and alarm sounds ship inside the app rather than being downloaded.

This is a property of how the app is built, not a promise about how we behave: there is no code in notsince that opens a network connection.

Permissions, and why each one is needed

Notifications
An alarm rings from a foreground service, which Android will not run without permission to post its notification. The same permission carries milestone and overdue-task reminders.
Alarms & reminders SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
So an alarm rings at the time you set, rather than whenever the system next decides to wake the app.
Full-screen notifications USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT
So a ringing alarm shows its screen over the lock screen instead of becoming a notification you have to find.
Vibrate, wake lock, run at startup
So an alarm can vibrate, wake the screen, and survive the phone being restarted.

None of these permissions are used to collect information about you. notsince does not request access to your files, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Children

notsince is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Settings contains Remove all data, which erases everything the app has stored. Uninstalling notsince also removes it. Because nothing is ever sent anywhere, there is nothing else to delete and nothing to request a copy of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The current version always ships inside the app, readable offline, under Settings › Privacy policy.
